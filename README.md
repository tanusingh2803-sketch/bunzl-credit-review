# Bunzl plc: Credit Review

Annual review of Bunzl plc's £1.25bn committed bank facilities (£950m syndicated RCF + £300m bilateral), written from the point of view of a lending bank in the syndicate. Data to 30 June 2026, with trading to 20 October 2026.

Author: Tanu Singh · October 2026

**Question:** Should the bank support the extension of its share of the facilities from 2030 to 2031, and on what terms?

**Answer:** Support, on the condition that both financial covenants are written into (or kept in) the bank facility agreements until final maturity. Covenant leverage is 1.5x (limit 3.5x), estimated interest cover is about 11.7x (minimum 3.0x), and liquidity is about £1.66bn net of overdrafts. Leverage is the binding covenant: it holds in the base case (about 2.5x by FY2028E) and through a sustained 15% EBITDA fall (the size of Bunzl's own severe case), but a sustained 20% EBITDA fall would breach it in FY2028E (3.7x) if buybacks and acquisitions carried on unchanged. Stopping them keeps leverage at about 2.3x.

## Key findings
| | FY2023 | FY2025 | LTM Jun-26 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue (£m) | 11,797 | 11,845 | 12,019 |
| Adjusted operating profit (£m) | 944 | 910 | 946 |
| Adjusted EBITDA, covenant basis, calculated (£m) | 963 | 931 | 969 |
| Net debt excl. leases (£m) | 1,086 | 1,664 | 1,432 |
| Covenant leverage, company reported (limit 3.5x) | 1.1x | 1.8x | 1.5x |
| Interest cover, our estimate (minimum 3.0x) | 15.6x | 11.3x | 11.7x |

- Earnings are stable to slightly lower, but net debt rose £578m between the end of FY2023 and the end of FY2025: over FY2024–FY2025, free cash flow of £1.21bn was outweighed by £1.76bn of dividends, buybacks and acquisitions.
- Liquidity is ample: £1,250m of undrawn committed facilities and about £1.66bn net of overdrafts. First bond maturity is October 2030; about £184m of USPPs repay by December 2027.
- Leverage, not interest cover, is the covenant to watch. In the base case it rises from 1.5x to about 2.5x by FY2028E as a £500m buyback and acquisitions exceed free cash flow, and adjusted net debt / EBITDA moves above the company's own 2.0–2.5x target from FY2027E.
- Stress test: in a sustained 20% EBITDA downside with no management action, leverage reaches 2.7x, 3.2x and 3.7x, breaching 3.5x in FY2028E. The largest sustained shock the covenant absorbs to FY2028E is about 16%, so a 15% fall, the size of Bunzl's own severe-but-plausible case, passes (3.4x). Stopping buybacks and M&A keeps the 20% case at about 2.3x. Interest cover stays above 5.8x throughout.
- Structural point: the USPPs issued in March 2022 contain a clause under which the principal covenants no longer apply once the earlier USPPs mature (latest 2028), before the 2031 bank maturity. Writing the covenants into the bank facilities is the main condition of the recommendation.

## Method
1. Spread three years of financials plus H1 2025, H1 2026 and LTM from the published reports, each input referenced to a page.
2. Mapped every facility, bond, maturity and covenant.
3. Built credit ratios and reconciled them to the company's own net debt and leverage figures. Bunzl does not disclose its interest cover, so EBITDA and interest cover are calculated and cross-checked to the reported 1.8x (FY2025) and 1.5x (LTM) leverage.
4. Forecast FY2026E–FY2028E with a base case and a 20% EBITDA downside (with and without management action), charging 5.5% on any borrowing above the base-case level; ran a 0–30% EBITDA shock sensitivity and static break-even points for both covenants.
5. Scored business and financial risk and wrote a credit paper with a recommendation and conditions.
